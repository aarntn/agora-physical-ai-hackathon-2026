# Judging scorecard
Official weights from handbook. Each judge rates 0–5; points = rating / 5 × weight; sum six then average judges. 0 absent, 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good for one-day build, 4 very good, 5 among event's best.
| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to produce | Actual rating (0–5) |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Physical AI & Technical Execution |25|Real sensor/device input, decision, physical response; architecture|TBD|
| Integration & Use of Agora Technologies |15|Live Agora voice/RTC, why central, actual event/tool use|TBD|
| Functionality & Reliability |15|Repeated end-to-end tests, failure recovery|TBD|
| Innovation & Creativity |15|Named comparators and demonstrable difference|TBD|
| Social Impact & Real-World Value |15|Specific user/problem, measured or honestly scoped benefit|TBD|
| Presentation & Demo |15|Clear full flow under two minutes; working hardware visible|TBD|

Tiebreak order: physical technical, reliability, Agora, then judges. Technical + reliability + Agora =55%. No track-specific weights. A rating of 4 on every criterion gives 80 points; not a qualification threshold. Target 4+ where feasible; only count demonstrated results.
