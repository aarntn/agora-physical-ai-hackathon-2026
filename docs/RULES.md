# Official competition brief
Source: organizer participant handbook screenshots supplied 27 Nov 2026. Event 28 Nov 2026.
- Build using Agora Conversational AI and/or RTC and ESP32-Mosaico kit.
- Choose exactly one award track at submission; cannot change after deadline.
- Social Impact: meaningful benefit to people, communities or environment.
- Best Innovation: unexpected combinations of physical AI, conversational AI and hardware.
- Best Human-AI Experience: help city council officers capture shop inspection evidence and findings (licence, hygiene, signboard); link each finding to supporting photo.
- Same six criteria for all tracks; see RUBRIC.md.
- Screening: two groups of 20 teams, 2–3 judges per group, about 3m15s per submission, no live pitch, code not run. Top 10 finalists. Live final slot: exactly five minutes (visible handbook footer; confirm pitch/Q&A split).
- One winner per track, no overall champion.
- Submit repository, README, demo video <=120 seconds, architecture overview, Agora explanation and selected track.
- Kits for event use only; prominent note says not for sale and cannot take home, overriding contradictory earlier sentence as working assumption; confirm with organizer.
- Open questions: exact submission deadline, permitted pre-written code, kit revision/modules/count, cloud credits, finalist time split, track eligibility specifics.
